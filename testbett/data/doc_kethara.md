# Kethara: A Gazetteer

## The planet

Kethara is the fourth planet orbiting the orange star Velis. A year on Kethara lasts 438 local days. A Ketharan day lasts 31 hours. Kethara has two moons. The larger moon is called Oro. Oro circles Kethara every 27 days. Thessa is the smaller moon. Thessa circles Kethara every 9 days. When both moons are full on the same night, Ketharans celebrate the Night of Two Moons. On the Night of Two Moons, families light blue lamps in their windows. The planet is slightly colder than Earth, and its gravity is about nine tenths of Earth's. Thin clouds of ice crystals often give the evening sky a pale green color. Because the day is so long, most Ketharans sleep twice: once after the midday meal and once late at night. The tilt of the planet's axis is small, so the seasons are mild everywhere except in the far north. Astronomers on Kethara keep two calendars, one for the planet's year and one for the cycles of its moons, and printed almanacs list both side by side.

## Geography

Kethara has two continents, Northreach and Solmara. Northreach is mostly tundra and pine forest, while Solmara has wide river valleys and most of the farmland. The capital, Amberfall, lies on the continent of Solmara. Amberfall is built on both banks of the Silverrun River. The Silverrun River flows west into the Pale Sea. Amberfall has a population of 2.3 million. The second largest city is Duskmere, a mining city. Duskmere lies in the Greyspine Mountains. The highest peak of the Greyspine Mountains is Mount Corrigan. Mount Corrigan rises to 9,140 meters. The Cinder Isles are a chain of volcanic islands in the Pale Sea. Station Merrow, a research outpost, stands on the largest of the Cinder Isles. Scientists at Station Merrow study the hot springs that bubble up along the shore. The Pale Sea is shallow near the coast and freezes along its northern shore in winter. Between the two continents lies the Narrow Strait, which is only 35 kilometers wide at its narrowest point. Most of the small towns of Northreach are linked by ferries and snow roads rather than by railways.

## History

The first settlers reached Kethara in 2311. They traveled aboard the ship Patient Wren. The Patient Wren was commanded by Aduke Farrow. Aduke Farrow was born on Halcyon, an orbital station near Ceres. The voyage from the inner system took nineteen years, and most of the 4,000 settlers slept through it in cold storage. The settlers signed the Charter of Lanterns in 2319. The Charter of Lanterns was signed in the town hall of Amberfall. The town hall has been called Charter Hall ever since. The first line of the Charter of Lanterns reads: "We came with little, and we will leave more than we found." In 2402 a dust storm known as the Long Dark covered the planet. The Long Dark lasted 70 days. During the Long Dark, the colony survived on stored grain and greenhouse lamps. The first decade had already been hard: crop failures in 2314 and 2315 forced the settlers to ration food, and the population grew slowly until new seed strains were developed. A second wave of settlers arrived in 2356 aboard three smaller ships.

## Government and law

Kethara is governed by the Council of Nine. Council members serve terms of six years. The current Speaker of the Council is Hollis Brandt. Hollis Brandt was born in Duskmere. Every councillor swears the Council Oath: "I will speak for those who are not in the room." The Height Law forbids any building in Amberfall taller than 40 meters. The Height Law exists because of the violent autumn windstorms. Under the Water Rule, no one may sell water drawn from the Silverrun River. At the age of 16, every citizen plants a tallow-tree on Rooting Day. Elections for the Council are held every spring for one third of the seats, and every citizen over the age of 18 may vote. A new law takes effect only after it has been read aloud in the main square of Amberfall.

## Economy

The currency of Kethara is the glim. One glim is divided into 100 shards. The main export of Kethara is ferrolite ore. Ferrolite ore is mined at Duskmere. The mayor of Duskmere is Rennick Oyelaran. The Spine Line railway connects Amberfall and Duskmere. The Spine Line is 1,260 kilometers long. The Spine Line opened in 2378. Kethara's spaceport, Farrow Port, stands on the Ithe Plateau. Farrow Port is named after Aduke Farrow. The motto painted over the departure gate of Farrow Port reads: "Every departure is a promise to return." Besides ferrolite, Kethara exports dried fish from the Pale Sea and timber from the pine forests of Northreach. Most trade with other worlds passes through a single orbital depot that circles high above the planet. Farms on Solmara grow a hardy grain called redwheat, which was bred during the early decades of the colony, and most households still bake their own bread.

## Life

The glassmoth is a native insect whose wings glow pale blue. The glassmoth feeds on the sap of the tallow-tree. Tallow-tree sap is burned as lamp fuel in rural villages. The largest native animal is the dune strider. The dune strider is a six-legged grazer that can weigh 900 kilograms. No intelligent native species has ever been found on Kethara. Most Ketharan plants are dark purple, a color that helps them absorb the weak light of their star. Dune striders live in herds on the dry plains of eastern Solmara and migrate north when the rains begin.

## Learning and culture

The Academy of Amberfall was founded in 2340. The Academy of Amberfall was founded by Ione Vasquez-Hale. Ione Vasquez-Hale was a geologist. Ione Vasquez-Hale made the first complete map of the Greyspine Mountains. The inscription above the door of Charter Hall reads: "What the wind cannot break, we will not bend." The anthem of Kethara is called "Two Lights Over Water". The traditional dish of Kethara is ember bread, which is baked in ash pits. Children attend school from the age of six. In winter, villages hold storytelling evenings in their halls, where elders retell the long voyage and the founding of the colony. Music is played mostly on reed pipes and frame drums. The most popular sport is ice sailing on the frozen northern shore of the Pale Sea, and every winter crews from both continents race there for a silver cup.
